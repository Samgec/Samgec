## Deep Patterns of the Top 10%

### 1. The hero is a price/economy promise — not a slogan
- Edvari: *"Economii de până la 90% la factura de energie"*
- Engie: *"Economie lunară de până la 100% la energia activă"*
- inoSOLAR: *"Energie curată pentru o viață"* + premium brands listed
- Samgec today: *"Mulți fac instalații. Doar că noi le facem foarte bine."* (no number, no benefit)

→ **Lead with a quantified outcome.** Romanians shopping for PV want a euro figure.

### 2. The funnel CTA is "Cere ofertă / Calculează economii" — not "Contact"
- Top sites all have a **two-CTA hero**: a strong primary ("Solicită ofertă" / "Calculează economii") plus a phone CTA.
- Engie has a full **price simulator** with monofazat/trifazat, consumption tier, AFM vs own funds. This is the single biggest conversion lift on the page.

### 3. Hardware brands are advertised explicitly
- inoSOLAR: *"panouri premium Longi, Canadian Solar, Jinko N-Type; invertoare Huawei, Sungrow, Solys, Deye, Victron"*
- Edvari: lists JA Solar, Huawei, SolarEdge, Longi by name in case studies.
- Engie: *"Sistem complet acumulatori Huawei LUNA2000"*

→ Buyers Google these brand names. Naming them is on-page SEO **and** trust signal.

### 4. The process is a numbered timeline (9 steps)
Edvari spells out 9 steps: Consultanță → Ofertă → Proiectare → Dosar ANRE → Aviz racordare → Montaj → Verificări → Conectare rețea → Punere în funcțiune. Each step has a duration. This single section closes the largest objection ("Is this complicated for me?").

### 5. ANRE / prosumator paperwork is reframed as "we handle it"
- Edvari: *"Tu te bucuri de economii — noi gestionăm documentele, avizele și termenele."*
- Engie: includes the dossier in the Solar package.
- This is the #1 friction in the Romanian buyer's mind: the paperwork. Top sites neutralize it loudly.

### 6. Case studies with hard numbers
Edvari shows real installations with: kWp, panel brand, inverter, annual production (kWh), euro economy, payback period. Engie quotes 12-year panel warranty, 10-year inverter warranty.

### 7. Warranty + financing are stated up-front
- Genway: 7–25 ani garanție
- Engie: rate 30% avans + 12/24/36 luni
- Siana: "fără avans, plată la montaj"
- inoSOLAR: 25 ani panou / 10 ani invertor / 5 ani montaj

### 8. Trust block: certifications + team photos
- inoSOLAR shows manager photos with names and roles.
- Engie cites ANRE + ISCIR licenses explicitly.
- SAMGEC has a *better* license list than most competitors but doesn't surface it.

### 9. Mobile-first form with WhatsApp shortcut
inoSOLAR has a dedicated WhatsApp form. EnergyFix has phone + WhatsApp floating buttons. Romanian PV buyers prefer phone/WhatsApp over email.

### 10. Long-form FAQ (8–12 questions, schema-marked)
Every top site has an FAQ covering: what is a prosumator, how long does it take, what does it cost, will it work in winter, do I need batteries, who handles ANRE paperwork, what's the Casa Verde grant, what maintenance is needed. FAQ schema = rich snippets in Google.

## What the Bottom 50% Do Badly
- Generic stock photos of solar panels (no own work).
- "Contact us" as the only CTA.
- No prices, no kWp examples, no timelines.
- Brand-name fonts but inconsistent type scale.
- Lorem Ipsum (samgec.ro/casa-verde — yes, really).
- Mobile menu broken, broken internal links (samgec referințe → încălzire în pardosea).

## Visual & Design Patterns of Top Sites
- **Palette:** Deep navy / teal / slate as primary; saturated yellow-orange OR cyan-blue as accent (sun = warmth) OR vibrant green (energy = eco). Edvari uses a near-black hero with bright blue accent. Engie uses blue + cyan. inoSOLAR uses sun-yellow.
- **Typography:** Geometric sans (Geist on Edvari, Lato/Roboto on Engie, Ubuntu on inoSOLAR). No serifs. Body 16–18px, h1 36–48px.
- **Imagery:** Big rooftop / panel-array hero photo, often a drone shot. Side product shots of inverter/battery hardware.
- **Animation:** Edvari uses Next.js + subtle scroll fades and timeline reveals; Engie static; inoSOLAR carousel. **None of the top 5 use cinematic GSAP-grade scroll animations** — this is the differentiation lane for SAMGEC.

## Niche Benchmarks (used in client report)
- Average Google rating across top 10: ~**4.7 / 5** (HomeRun, Necesit, Google Business listings).
- Average warranty offered: **10 ani invertor / 12 ani panou / 25 ani producție** for premium tier; 5 ani for low-tier.
- Typical residential payback: **5–7 ani**.
- Typical residential system size advertised: **3–8 kWp**.
- Average advertised reduction: **70–90% bill reduction**, up to 100% with batteries.
