# Client Brand Extraction — SAMGEC SRL

## Brand Snapshot
- **Company:** SC SAMGEC SRL (Satu Mare)
- **Founded:** 1994 (30+ years in operation)
- **Primary Color:** `#233D63` (deep navy — trust/industrial)
- **Secondary Color:** `#6C757D` (slate grey — utility/engineering)
- **Accent Color:** `#FF5E13` (vibrant orange — energy, CTA)
- **Background:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Fonts (current):** Barlow (heading) / Rubik / Roboto (body)
- **Tone:** Formal, blue-collar, regional, trust-oriented. Reads like an industrial trade firm that hasn't updated its messaging since ~2010.
- **Core Message:** *"SAMGEC – partener pentru o viață"* — Mulți fac instalații. Doar că noi le facem foarte bine. ("Many do installations. We just do them very well.")
- **Logo:** https://samgec.ro/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/logo1.png (downloaded → `site/assets/logo.png`)
- **Favicon:** downloaded → `site/assets/favicon.png`
- **Site stack:** WordPress 6.6.5 + Elementor 4.0.7 + JupiterX theme — performance is poor and design is dated.

## Tone of Voice
- Formal Romanian, slightly old-school. Heavy use of legal/technical vocabulary (autorizație, atestat, prescripție tehnică, ISCIR, ANRE).
- Pride-driven: emphasizes 40+ employees, 30+ years, certifications, regional embedding in Satu Mare.
- Weakness: no emotional hook for the homeowner buying photovoltaics today. The site speaks like a contractor to another contractor, not to a family planning to halve their electricity bill.
- Quality values stated explicitly: **Seriozitate, Calitate, Siguranță, Profesionalism**.
